# Payworks Source Material — staged design-partner records

The anonymized design-partner transcript set, staged for filing. **Not raw material yet** —
these files are inbox staging: `/process-meeting` moves each one to the central archive at
`product/user-insights/transcripts/{date}-{account}-{type}.md` and writes its summary. The
folder trends toward empty.

**Read this when:** You need the source behind a claim on an account or initiative page
before the records have been filed, or you are about to process them.
