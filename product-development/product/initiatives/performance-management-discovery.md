# Performance Management Discovery (HR 1.0)

## Snapshot

- Discovery for the Performance Management module — HR 1.0 on the roadmap, and one of the two retention-floor modules the FY27 test measures. Today the product carries review *documentation* inside Human Resources; this initiative is about whether Payworks builds the review *process*.
- **Thirteen design-partner records across four accounts**, run between 2026-06-22 and 2026-08-12: six Northwind discovery interviews plus two Northwind client calls, one Maplewood interview, two Cascadia sessions, and two Harbourview sessions. Twelve are discovery interviews or prototype reviews; the 2026-08-05 Northwind session is a **reverse demo** — the customer walking us through their current workflow, which is a different kind of evidence and should be read as such.
- Every one of the four accounts runs performance management **outside Payworks today** — QuickBase at Northwind, DocuSign at Harbourview, PDF forms on an intranet at Maplewood, Word documents and paper at Cascadia. Nobody is starting from nothing, and every one of them is displacing something.
- The prototype has been in front of managers at three levels (director, site manager, front-line supervisor) and in front of HR at all four accounts. Reactions are positive on substance and consistently nervous about density — *"The adoption would be at risk here, because there is so much going on."*
- "Done" for discovery = a synthesis that a Product Brief can be written from, with the contradictions named rather than averaged.

## Scope & goal

- **Goal:** close the **39% Human Resources gap** in win-loss exits and defend the **94%+ retention** floor, by establishing whether a Payworks-native review process wins against the incumbents these customers already run — and what it must do to displace them.
- **In scope:** the review cycle and its cadence; ratings, scales and weighting; goals and check-ins; the manager/employee/HR/executive surfaces; and the handoff from a completed review into compensation and payroll, which is where every account bleeds time.
- **Out of scope:** compensation management as a module (HR 4.0), learning management (HR 5.0), and recruiting (HR 3.0).
- **The contradictions to carry into definition, not resolve prematurely:** peer and 360 feedback is wanted by Northwind's managers and declined by Northwind's HR Manager, who is the buyer; the operations director wants weighted category scoring while the site supervisor wants a flat average; Harbourview's increases are automatic and rating-independent while Cascadia's are individually CEO-approved and Maplewood's are set by executive off a board-approved budget with the review feeding only the bonus. There is no single compensation model to build against.

## Instructions

- Thirteen records, four accounts, one module — read across accounts, never by folder. Design partners contradict each other (peer feedback, weighted vs flat scoring, and three incompatible compensation models). Report the divergence; do not smooth it. Prototype screen values quoted inside the records are Payworks mock data, not customer facts.
