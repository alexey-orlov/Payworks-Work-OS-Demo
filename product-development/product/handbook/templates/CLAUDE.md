# Templates

Blank templates with bracketed placeholders. Copy, do not edit in place.

**Read this when:** You are starting a new PRD, launch checklist, retro, or customer interview.

Living master files are *not* here: stakeholder profiles live in `../../strategy/business-context/stakeholders.md`, quarterly OKRs in `../../strategy/current-quarter.md`, company fundamentals in `../../strategy/business-context/business-info.md`. Those are filled once and edited in place — the opposite of a template.

## Contents

### Files

- [interview-template.md](interview-template.md) — Customer interview scaffold: pre-interview checklist, Mom-Test-style question bank, debrief format
- [launch-checklist-template.md](launch-checklist-template.md) — Launch checklist scaffold; filled copies go to `../../launches/{initiative-slug}-launch-checklist.md`, one per initiative (launches are launches of initiatives)
- [prd-template.md](prd-template.md) — **Product Brief** scaffold (house format): the 8 SOP sections plus Overview, UX Principles and Rollout, with the three-tier KPI Framework; serves both levels — Product Brief (Initiative) and VP Product Brief (Value Package); copies go to `../../PRDs/{area}/{initiative-slug}-product-brief.md`; `/product-brief-draft` builds on it
- [retrospective-template.md](retrospective-template.md) — Sprint/project retro scaffold: what went well, what didn't, action items
- [initiative-page-template.md](initiative-page-template.md) — Living initiative page scaffold; copies go to `../../initiatives/{slug}.md`, one per initiative, edited in place
- [competitor-teardown-template.md](competitor-teardown-template.md) — Living competitor profile scaffold; copies go to `../../competitive-research/competitors/{slug}/teardown.md`, one per competitor, edited in place
- [competitive-area-matrix-template.md](competitive-area-matrix-template.md) — Per-area capability matrix scaffold; copies go to `../../competitive-research/competitive-matrix-{area}.md` when an area outgrows the main matrix
- [micro-jobs-breakdown-template.md](micro-jobs-breakdown-template.md) — **Micro Jobs Breakdown** scaffold (house format): backbone, per-job Problem Statements with evidence labels, the sequenced MJ table, pressure-test record, coverage check; copies go to `../../PRDs/{area}/{initiative-slug}-micro-jobs-breakdown.md`; `/micro-jobs-breakdown` builds on it
- [job-spec-template.md](job-spec-template.md) — **Micro Job** scaffold (house format): the buildable contract for one MJ — happy path, alternate flows, named exceptions, edge cases, acceptance criteria, telemetry plan, exception list, pressure test; copies go to `../../PRDs/{area}/{initiative-slug}-mj-{NN}-{job-slug}-micro-job.md`; `/micro-job-draft` builds on it
- [account-context-template.md](account-context-template.md) — Living account page scaffold; copies go to `../../customers/accounts/{account-slug}/account-context.md`, one per account, edited in place
