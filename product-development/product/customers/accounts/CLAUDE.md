# Accounts

**Read this when:** You are looking up a specific account.

## Contents

### Files

- [portfolio.yaml](portfolio.yaml) — Account health registry (status, ARR, renewal, risks, expansion signals, and segment fields — vertical, size band, use cases — per account); read by `/portfolio-pulse`, updated by `/process-meeting` and `/context-update`, rolled up into `strategy/business-context/segmentation-matrix.md`

- [acme-corp/](acme-corp/) — Synthetic 75-employee account carrying the Administrator and Manager personas — the small, uncomplicated customer voice; no transcripts
- [birchbark-books/](birchbark-books/) — Synthetic bookkeeping practice with ~40 SMB clients, carrying the Accountant and Bookkeeper personas and the accountant-and-bookkeeper channel; no transcripts
