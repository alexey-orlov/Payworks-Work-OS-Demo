# [Employee Self Service] Manager capability on the mobile app, starting with note capture after a conversation

Their managers are mobile by the nature of the work — installers and shop crews across
multiple branches — and today's Payworks app is employee-scoped, so a manager cannot record
anything from it. What they are trying to achieve is not a manager app in the abstract:
it is capturing the agreement at the moment the conversation ends, with a date on it,
visible to both the employee and HR, instead of the conversation becoming an email to HR
that HR then re-types into the employee file and the Discipline tab.

This is the account's **named adoption gate** for the Performance Management module. Asked
straight out whether manager mobile would encourage adoption, the answer was
*"Yeah, yeah, a lot, a lot."*

She drew the boundary herself: note capture yes, formal discipline no — serious warning
wording is prepared sitting down and *"they usually go through me."*

*"Because you mentioned that there will be an app for managers. Right now, app is strictly for recording times, or time of requests, or whatever, so managers cannot do their stuff. Because a lot of our managers are constantly on the go." — Their HR Lead*

*"Take some notes, right away on the phone, or even just record, like." — Their HR Lead*

*"Because it will save the date, and we just had a conversation, we discussed this, we agreed this, and just click save, and the employee can see it, and I can see it goes under the file, that some notes." — Their HR Lead*

**Today instead:** the manager emails HR; HR saves the email as a PDF into the employee
file and separately creates a Discipline-tab record. **Signal strength:** highest in the
session — volunteered unprompted, then confirmed on a direct probe, and framed as the
condition under which managers would actually use the feature at all.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Give managers a mobile surface for performance note capture so a
conversation is recorded where it happened, not re-keyed by HR afterwards.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- A manager can create a dated note against a direct report from the mobile app in under a minute.
- The note carries the conversation date automatically.
- The manager chooses whether the employee can see the note; the default is stated explicitly.
- The note lands on the same employee record the web surface reads, with no HR step in between.
- Formal disciplinary documents are explicitly out of scope for the mobile surface.
