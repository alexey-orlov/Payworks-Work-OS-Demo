# [Human Resources] Role profiles in the platform — key competencies, responsibilities and credentials, visible from the review

The strongest positive reaction in the session, and it was unprompted. Every employee at
this account signs a job description on joining, and HR maintains those documents by hand
outside Payworks. Her argument is that the review is only meaningful if manager and
employee are looking at the same definition of the role first — she has repeatedly seen the
two disagree about what the job even is. Seeing role profile and key competencies on the
prototype's employee surface is what she said she wanted most.

She also expects the role profile to carry credentials alongside responsibilities and
competencies — the same certification-tracking problem this account is already known for.

*"Yeah, I can see, like, first of all, what I see, it's good, it's like, I can see my role profile." / "and my key competencies, which I really like." — Their HR Lead / HR Coordinator*

*"Yeah, this is… this… I would love to have it in the platform! It's, like, so cool!" — Their HR Lead / HR Coordinator*

*"But employee thinks completely different. And job description, it's something that puts them on the same page." — Their HR Lead*

*"Oh, I don't know yet, like, because we don't use anything, like, job descriptions in paywalks yet, but I guess there should be something if we are going to work with roles or positions." — Their HR Lead*

*"sees responsibilities, and I already mentioned also credentials." — Their HR Lead*

**Today instead:** signed job description documents maintained by HR outside the platform,
which she tells managers to print before a review. **Signal strength:** high — the clearest
positive pull of the session, and it maps onto an existing, costly manual practice.
**Prototype gap found alongside it:** from the role profile there was no way back — she
asked how to return, twice, from two screens.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Hold role/position profiles in the platform — responsibilities, key
competencies and required credentials — and surface them to both employee and manager from
inside the review.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- A position carries a description, key responsibilities, key competencies and required credentials.
- The employee can open their own role profile from their performance surface.
- The manager sees the same profile while writing the review.
- A competency shows what it means in practice, not just its name.
- Changing an employee's position updates which profile they see.
- There is an obvious way back to the previous screen from the profile.
