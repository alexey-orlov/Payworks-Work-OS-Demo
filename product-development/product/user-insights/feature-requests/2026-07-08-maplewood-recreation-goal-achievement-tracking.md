# [Performance Management] Goal-achievement tracking — close the loop on goals that are set and never measured

**Who asked:** their HR Business Partner, on the prototype's goals screen (2026-07-08).

**Underlying need.** They use SMART goals and set them collaboratively during the review
meeting — and then nothing measures them. Whether a goal was achieved is decided informally at
the next year's review, by whatever the leader remembers. She named this gap the moment the
prototype showed goal achievement.

*"This might be… really helpful for us to measure goal achievement. Right now, we don't… we don't measure goal achievement. It's really goal setting, although we use SMART goals, it's really between the employee and the leader to determine what the goals are, and if they've been achieved, during the next performance review. But this would be really, neat to keep track of, are those goals actually being achieved?" — Their HR Business Partner*

*"Yeah, there's no… there's no loop, closing the loop with goals, with our goals right now." — Their HR Business Partner*

**What they do today instead.** Goals are written into the review document and revisited a year
later from memory. Goals are also edited mid-year, and their fiscal-year window (May execution)
means employees and managers do not even share a consistent goal period.

**Signal strength.** Strong, with a second reason behind it: their executive already calculates
director bonuses as a percentage of salary *based on goal achievement*, so achievement data
exists somewhere at the executive level and neither participant has sight of it. Closing this
loop connects the review to a decision that is already being made.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Track a goal's achievement state through its period, so the next review reads
recorded outcomes rather than recollection.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- A goal carries an achievement state and a manager rating of achievement at review time.
- Goals can be added or edited mid-period without breaking the record of what was agreed.
- A goal's period is configurable and can follow a fiscal year rather than the calendar year.
- Achievement history for prior periods is visible from the current review.
