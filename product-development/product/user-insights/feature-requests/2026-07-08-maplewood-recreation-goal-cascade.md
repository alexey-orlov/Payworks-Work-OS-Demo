# [Performance Management] Goal cascade from organisational to director to employee goals

**Who asked:** their HR Business Partner, in response to Margaret Foster raising company-goal
cascading as something the account probably did not do (2026-07-08).

**Underlying need.** They have organisational goals and they coach leaders on the idea that a
personal goal should ladder up through the director's goals to the organisation's — but it does
not actually happen that way, and their goal setting is inconsistent as a result. The cascade
would give the ladder a structure instead of a coaching conversation.

*"We do have organizational goals, and then the way, like, when we're kind of… because we… our role is we kind of coach the… the leaders through this process as well. You have the goals. The organizational goals, but then you have your… like, your next level leader goals, right? Like, in my case, I have my director's goals, which should cascade down to my goals. you know, my goals should speak to the director's goals, that should speak to the organizational goals, so that is actually, something that is top of mind. It's a new kind of concept for us, and does it happen that way? No. we're a bit inconsistent with… with goal setting." — Their HR Business Partner*

*"But it would be actually quite nice… I think our executive would quite like that kind of cascade down ability, for the… to see that in the goals section." — Their HR Business Partner*

**What they do today instead.** Nothing systematic. Goals are agreed between employee and
leader during the review meeting; the ladder to organisational goals exists as an aspiration
they coach toward.

**Signal strength.** Moderate, and notable for how it arrived: Margaret introduced it as
something they likely would *not* use, and it drew the most forward-looking response of the
call — *"that is actually, something that is top of mind."* But the interest is attributed to
their executive, who was not in the room. Treat as an appetite to validate with the Employee
Experience Director, not a confirmed requirement.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Let goals reference a parent goal, so organisational goals can cascade through
leader goals down to employee goals, with the ladder visible from any level.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- A goal can be linked to a parent goal at a higher level of the organisation.
- An employee sees which higher-level goal their goal supports.
- Cascade depth is configurable, and cascading can be turned off entirely.
- Employee-set goals with no parent remain valid.
