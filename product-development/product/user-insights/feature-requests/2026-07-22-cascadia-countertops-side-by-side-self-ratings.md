# [Performance Management] Show the employee's self-rating to the manager while they rate

She completed a self-assessment in the employee seat, moved to the manager seat, and could
not find it. In their real process the comparison *is* the exercise: managers hand out the
form, collect it, and compare. Without the self-rating in view, collecting it has no
purpose — and, in her account of it, agreement between the two ratings is what makes the
acknowledgement conversation short.

*"Oh, maybe it was here somewhere, but here I could not see the self-assessment." — Their HR Lead*

*"Of course, this is the main point of self-assessment," — Their HR Lead*

*"It will ease the acknowledgement processes. If we agree on something, then boom, boom, boom, done. But if we have differences in opinion, then it's good to read these comments." — Their HR Lead*

*"They just give them this form, pre-in to send, and say, can you assess yourself? And then they assess them, and then they compare the results. Not everybody, but yeah, that's good." — Their HR Lead*

**Today instead:** two paper or Word forms compared side by side by the manager.
**Signal strength:** high, and partly a prototype gap rather than a missing concept — one
manager screen did show a self-rating, the competency section did not. Facilitator
acknowledged it in session as a likely prototype gap.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Make the employee's self-rating and self-comment visible to the manager on
every rated item while the manager rates.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- Every rated item (goal and competency) shows the employee's self-rating beside the manager's input.
- The employee's comment for that item is visible with it.
- Divergence between self and manager rating is visually distinguishable at a glance.
- The manager's own rating is never pre-filled from the self-rating.
