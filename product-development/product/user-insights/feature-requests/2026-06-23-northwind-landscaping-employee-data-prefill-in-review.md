# [Performance Management] Pull existing Payworks employee data into the review

**Who asked:** Their Operations Director at Northwind Landscaping, in a discovery interview + prototype review on 2026-06-23. He raised it in his first substantive answer, unprompted, before any prototype was shown, and returned to it repeatedly.

**The underlying need.** Their review system holds no data of its own, so preparing a review is memory work plus a hunt through other systems. Meanwhile the facts that would ground a rating — attendance, documented performance issues, letters of expectation — are already filed in Payworks. He wants the review to arrive pre-loaded with what the system already knows, both quantitative and qualitative, and he wants the same evidence visible to whoever approves the review above him.

**What they do today.** He maintains a private running Word document of milestones, kudos and issues on each direct report, written as things occur, specifically so he does not have to go looking later. Colleagues without that discipline fall back on recall. Performance issues and letters of expectation do get filed into Payworks — but nothing carries them into the review.

*"if they could pull that information in advance, that would be a very beneficial and time-saving approach."* — Their Operations Director

*"It doesn't really collect data."* — Their Operations Director

*"That would be helpful as well, especially if we could put it right into Payworks, right? Like, so if there's, you know, maybe a quarterly review that's done, maybe we put that into Payworks under their file, and that Payworks then pulls that into the performance review and makes it readily available for the manager doing the review."* — Their Operations Director

*"Because we're already doing something similar in Payworks as it is, right? So if there's a letter of expectations, or there's a performance issue, we put it into Payworks."* — Their Operations Director

**Signal strength: strong.** Unprompted opening ask, restated at least four times across both halves of the session, and it is the concrete form of the account's headline consolidation ask. It is also the answer to his stated real constraint — time, not clicks.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Surface the employee data Payworks already holds inside the review being written — attendance, documented performance issues, disciplinary records and letters of expectation — available to the reviewer and to the approver above them.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- A review shows an evidence panel drawing on the employee's existing Payworks record for the review period.
- Attendance data for the review period is available without leaving the review.
- Documented performance issues, letters of expectation and disciplinary records filed in Payworks are listed for the review period, each linking to the source document.
- Free-text notes filed against the employee record during the period (for example a quarterly check-in) are pulled into the review.
- The same evidence is visible to anyone reviewing the review before it is processed.
- Which sources appear is configurable, since the appropriate evidence differs by review level.
- Nothing is auto-written into the rating or comments — the evidence informs the reviewer, it does not fill the form.
