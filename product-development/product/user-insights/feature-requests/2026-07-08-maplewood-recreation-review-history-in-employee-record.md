# [Performance Management] Prior reviews, development plans and letters of expectation surfaced on the review

**Who asked:** both participants, independently — their HR Business Partner on the prototype's
employee dashboard, their Payroll Manager immediately after (2026-07-08).

**Underlying need.** High leader turnover means the person writing a review frequently does not
know the employee's performance-management history, and does not go looking for it. Their
development plans and letters of expectation are Word documents that end up in the employee
self-service file, and prior reviews are uploaded there too — but retrieval is painful enough
that nobody does it before a review. They want that history visible from the review surface, for
the employee and the manager both.

*"Especially within our organization, we have lots of leader turnover, and sometimes they don't do their own research. into an employee file before they do a performance review, so they might not even know that the person has a development plan, or they have a letter of expectation on file." — Their HR Business Partner*

*"Sorry, I had a lot of managers recently ask for copies of the previous year's performance review so that they can start preparing for the next one." — Their Payroll Manager*

*"the Payroll Manager's uploading the performance reviews into the employee self-serve, but we have to go into the documents library, and we have to kind of click and click and click and click until we actually find it." — Their HR Business Partner*

**What they do today instead.** Documents are uploaded into the employee self-service document
library and carefully labelled by the Payroll Manager; managers ask her for copies rather than
digging.

**Signal strength.** Strong — the only ask in this session raised independently by both
participants, and it doubles as the "one-stop shop" argument for keeping performance inside
Payworks at all. Cross-module: the documents live in Human Resources and surface through
Employee Self Service, so this is a linking requirement, not a storage one.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Surface an employee's performance-management history — prior reviews, active
development plans, letters of expectation — from the employee and manager review views, without
a trip to the document library.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- The employee's performance view lists their prior reviews and any active development plan or letter of expectation.
- The manager sees the same history for a direct report before starting a review.
- Documents are linked from wherever they already live, never duplicated.
- Visibility respects the confidentiality rules that apply to the underlying document.
