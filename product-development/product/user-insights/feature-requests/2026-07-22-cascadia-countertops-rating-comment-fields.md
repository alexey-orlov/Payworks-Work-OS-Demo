# [Performance Management] A comment field on every rating, for the employee and the manager

Raised from both seats in the same session. As the employee she wanted to explain a rating
she had given herself; as the manager she wanted to read the employee's explanation and add
her own. A bare number carries no reason, and in her experience the reason is what makes a
low rating discussable rather than disputable — the cause is often circumstantial
(a broken system, an overload) rather than the person.

*"I know maybe it's a lot to ask, but it would be awesome if there would be an option to leave a comment." — Their HR Lead / HR Coordinator*

*"It's a very simple field where I can type a few words, if I need it." — Their HR Lead / HR Coordinator*

*"I would rate myself, because for some reasons, it's not working. So this is the good thing, and it's always, like, in any reviews, if I give, especially negative." — Their HR Lead / HR Coordinator*

*"And again, I would like to… if… if he would say…" / "left any comments, it would be great, or I can leave any comments." — Their HR Lead / HR Coordinator*

**Today instead:** the Word review form has free-text sections, so this exists in their
current process and would be lost in the move to the product. **Signal strength:** high —
asked twice, unprompted, from two different personas. The "a lot to ask" preface is
politeness; she then described it as something she wants in *any* review.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Attach an optional free-text comment to every individual rating, on both the
self-assessment and the manager assessment.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- Each rated item accepts a short free-text comment from the rater.
- The comment saves with the draft and survives submission.
- Manager and employee comments for the same item are displayed together on the completed review.
- Comments are optional — a rating without one still submits.
