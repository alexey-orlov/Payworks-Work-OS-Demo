# [Performance Management] Structured recognition feedback with a date, and constructive feedback routed through HR first

Customers email praise for install crews to a branch manager or showroom supervisor, and
that praise is evidence at review time. Today HR saves each email as a PDF into the
employee's file by hand. What she wants is for the person who received the praise to record
it themselves, in one structured place, so it is there when the review happens.

She split the visibility model without being asked. Positive feedback can be open. Anything
constructive must reach HR before it reaches anyone else — it may be an early progressive-
discipline signal, it may be a harassment concern, or it may be unfounded and say more
about the person raising it. She added one concrete requirement: every entry needs a
visible date, because feedback from a year ago should not weigh like feedback from
last month.

*"Request feedback from someone. This is, like, awesome!" — Their HR Lead / HR Coordinator*

*"And it would be nice to keep this somewhere structured in the same place, so what, like, the showroom manager can do. Right now, I'm just saving the mail as PDF and loading in the employee's file." — Their HR Lead*

*"If it is a positive feedback, I think… Anybody can see it." — Their HR Lead*

*"But I don't think that maybe only managers should see it. If somebody expresses concerns, actually, it should go to HR. And then they can allow the manager to see it." — Their HR Lead*

*"Okay? And the only thing that I really would like to see it is the date." — Their HR Lead*

**Today instead:** HR converts praise emails to PDF and uploads them to the employee file —
*"But it's a lot of work"*. **Signal strength:** enthusiastic but not gating — she never
framed it as a condition of adoption, so it is filed nice-to-have despite the strong
reaction and the real manual cost. **Divergence note for the synthesis:** peer feedback is
contested across this corpus — record this as one account's HR function wanting it *with*
a routing rule, not as blanket support.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Let anyone record structured feedback about an employee, dated, with
positive feedback openly visible and constructive feedback routed to HR before release.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- Any employee can leave feedback about any employee, and can request feedback.
- Every entry shows the date it was given.
- Feedback is typed positive or constructive at entry.
- Positive feedback is visible to the employee and their manager by default.
- Constructive feedback is visible to HR only until HR releases it to the manager.
- Feedback for the review period appears to the manager while writing the review.
