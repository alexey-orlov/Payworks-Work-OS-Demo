# [Performance Management] Quarterly or semi-annual review cadence for field crews

Northwind Landscaping's **site supervisor** intends to repeat the review round he ran off his
own initiative, and when asked how often it would be valuable he named **quarterly or every
six months** — not annual. Two reasons, both his own: crew turnover means an annual cycle can
miss a person's whole tenure, and he does not want anyone learning about a problem late.

The underlying need is a cycle short enough that the record still describes the person who is
standing in front of you, and frequent enough that the review is a temperature check rather
than a verdict. Note that this sits against the account's formal cadence — a mid-year
checkpoint plus an annual review, mid-year being optional and under-adopted.

*"It's… it's hard, because there… there can be a turnover here. So, I mean, depending on the
rollover, I would say either quarterly or, uh, every half year kind of deal, every 6 months, I
think would be enough."* — Their Site Supervisor

*"But, like, just for me, I'm the type of person where…"* / *"I like to know if I'm messing up
earlier, then all of a sudden I'm going, hey, you've screwed up here, now you're in trouble. I
like to know, like, right away, when I do it, let me know. So that's kind of also where it came
from too, right, is just touching base, doing a temperature check with the crew."* — Their Site
Supervisor

*"Yeah, I, I like to, yeah, I think, I thought it was fun, like I said, it brought morale up for
a while, everyone was joking"* — Their Site Supervisor (on making it a regular process)

**Signal strength.** He gave a range rather than a number, in answer to a direct question, so
this is a preference rather than a demand. Its value is corroborative: a front-line manager
volunteering that he would run reviews **more** often than his company requires is unusual and
worth weighing against the assumption that cadence increases are a burden managers resist.

**What they do today:** the company runs a mid-year checkpoint and an annual review; mid-year
is optional and under-adopted. His own round was a one-off, outside that cycle, unscheduled.

## Draft ticket

**Objective:** Support review cycles configured at quarterly and semi-annual cadences alongside
annual, so a team whose workforce turns over can run reviews that land inside people's tenure.

**Acceptance criteria seed:**
- Cycle frequency is configurable per review programme — annual, semi-annual, quarterly — not
  hard-coded to an annual calendar.
- A cycle can be started for a subset of employees (one crew, one site) without launching the
  company-wide round.
- New hires joining mid-cycle are handled explicitly: either enrolled in the current cycle or
  deferred, by a stated rule rather than by silence.
- A shorter cycle does not multiply the paperwork of a long one — a quarterly instance should be
  lighter than an annual, or the cadence request defeats itself.
- Prior cycles remain visible on the employee record so cycle-over-cycle movement is readable.
